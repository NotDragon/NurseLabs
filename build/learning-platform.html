Platform Overview

The NURSE-LABS training programme is delivered through a dedicated Learning Management System. The platform translates the validated European Competence Framework into structured digital learning, simulation-based practice, assessment and competence tracking.

Access the Learning Platform

Features

  • Structured learning modules
  • Clinical learning scenarios
  • Glaucoma, AMD and diabetic-retinopathy content
  • At least 8 interactive clinical simulations
  • Formative and summative assessments
  • Competence tracking
  • Learner progression monitoring
  • Learning analytics
  • Digital badges / micro-credentials
  • Educator and facilitator resources
  • Community of Practice access when launched
