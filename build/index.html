NURSE-LABS

Digital Training for Nurses on Chronic Vision Care and Medication Adherence

Advancing nursing competence in chronic vision care through evidence, simulation and European collaboration.

NURSE-LABS develops and validates a European competence-based vocational training model for nurses supporting people with chronic vision conditions, including glaucoma, age-related macular degeneration and diabetic retinopathy.

About the Project

NURSE-LABS addresses a European training gap in chronic vision counselling and adherence support. The project brings together clinical, nursing, academic, health literacy and digital education expertise to develop a validated European Competence Framework, an EQF-aligned curriculum and a simulation-based digital learning ecosystem. The model is designed to strengthen measurable counselling competences, support continuing professional development and provide a scalable approach for vocational nursing education across Europe.

Project Objectives

European Competence Framework

Define 10-12 BIG IDEAS and measurable competences for chronic vision counselling and adherence support.

3-Round Delphi Validation

Validate the competence framework through structured transnational expert consensus.

EQF-Aligned Curriculum

Translate validated competences into a modular, outcome-based curriculum aligned with European qualification principles.

Digital Simulation

Operationalise the curriculum through an LMS-based learning ecosystem with interactive clinical simulations and competence tracking.

Transnational Pilot

Pilot the training in real educational and professional settings and measure competence development through pre- and post-assessment.

Impact & Sustainability

Translate project evidence into CPD pathways, policy recommendations, long-term digital access and a European Community of Practice.
