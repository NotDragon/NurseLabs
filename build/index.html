NURSE-LABS

About the Project

NURSE-LABS was developed to support nurses working with chronic vision care, where adherence, counselling and patient confidence directly affect outcomes. The project is supported by Erasmus+ and delivered by a seven-partner consortium across Cyprus, Greece, Italy and Poland. It combines ophthalmology expertise, European vocational education, digital learning design and simulation-based training to create a practical, evidence-informed programme for nurses.
